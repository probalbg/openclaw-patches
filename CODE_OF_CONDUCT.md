# Code of Conduct

We want a respectful, useful technical community.

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Share reproducible evidence and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or expose private information about others.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its technical scope.

Maintainers may edit, close, or remove contributions that violate these expectations. Serious or repeated violations may result in being blocked from project spaces.
